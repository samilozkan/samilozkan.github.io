<p class="about-lead">Artificial intelligence is changing how we build software and work with data. This page brings together practical ways to think about AI in data engineering, from useful applications to the foundations that make them reliable.</p>

<h2 class="section-label"><span>//</span> AI in the data workflow</h2>

<div class="stages">
  <div class="stage">
    <span class="stage-no">01</span>
    <h3>Prepare the data</h3>
    <p>AI systems depend on data that is well-modeled, documented, access-controlled, and fit for its intended use.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="stage">
    <span class="stage-no">02</span>
    <h3>Build useful context</h3>
    <p>Reliable pipelines and clear metadata help make the right information available to the right applications.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="stage">
    <span class="stage-no">03</span>
    <h3>Evaluate the result</h3>
    <p>Test outputs against real requirements, monitor quality over time, and keep people accountable for important decisions.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<h2 class="section-label"><span>//</span> principles</h2>

<ul class="exploring">
  <li>Start with a clear problem and measure whether AI improves the outcome.</li>
  <li>Protect sensitive data and follow the policies that apply to the system and its users.</li>
  <li>Validate generated answers and code before relying on them in production.</li>
  <li>Design for transparency, human oversight, and graceful failure.</li>
</ul>
